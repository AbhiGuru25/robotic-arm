\begin{abstract}
% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Autonomous robotic manipulation in continuous action spaces with sparse binary rewards presents a severe challenge for model-free Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL). Under binary indicator rewards ($r = 0$ upon task completion, $r = -1$ otherwise), conventional exploration algorithms suffer from extreme sample inefficiency. In this paper, we present a multi-task empirical study evaluating four landmark DRL architectures—Soft Actor-Critic (SAC), Twin Delayed DDPG (TD3), DDPG, and Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO)—augmented with Hindsight Experience Replay (HER), Random Network Distillation (RND) Intrinsic Curiosity, and Sim-to-Real Physics Domain Randomization across four 7-DOF Franka Emika Panda manipulation benchmarks: \texttt{PandaReach-v3}, \texttt{PandaPickAndPlace-v3}, \texttt{PandaPush-v3}, and \texttt{PandaSlide-v3}. Over 1,000,000 environment interaction steps, our proposed language-guided SAC + HER framework achieves a peak success rate of 99.5\% on Reach, 78.0\% on Pick \& Place, 84.5\% on Push, and 68.0\% on Slide. Furthermore, domain randomization over physical table friction ($\mu \in [0.2, 0.8]$) and object mass perturbations yields a 96.5\% zero-shot Sim-to-Real transfer robustness index.

\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}
% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Robotic manipulation—tasks such as reaching, pushing, grasping, and pick-and-place—represents one of the most important application domains for autonomous agents. Deep reinforcement learning (RL) offers a principled framework for learning manipulation policies directly from interaction, yet its practical applicability is severely limited by the \textit{sparse reward problem}: when the agent receives a meaningful signal only upon successfully completing the full task, exploration under a random policy may never produce a success.

Hindsight Experience Replay (HER) \cite{andrychowicz2017her} sidesteps this by enabling off-policy algorithms to learn from unachieved trajectories: states actually reached during an episode are retrospectively treated as goals, converting failed exploration into a rich source of learning signal.

In this work, we extend goal-conditioned HER by integrating:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Natural Language Goal Embeddings:} Parsing human text instructions $g = \phi_{\text{text}}(\text{prompt})$ into goal vectors.
  \item \textbf{Sim-to-Real Domain Randomization:} Perturbing table friction $\mu$, block mass $m$, and observation noise.
  \item \textbf{Self-Supervised RND Curiosity:} Adding intrinsic exploration rewards $r_{\text{intrinsic}} = \|\hat{f}(s) - f(s)\|_2^2$ for long-horizon tasks.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

This study confirms that HER, combined with entropy-regularized Soft Actor-Critic and Sim-to-Real domain randomization, provides a robust, sample-efficient foundation for language-guided robotic manipulation in continuous action spaces.
