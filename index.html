How It Works: Key Technical Concepts

Understanding sparse binary rewards and Hindsight Experience Replay (HER)

🎯

1. Sparse Binary Reward

The robot receives reward r = 0.0 only when the block touches the target goal within 5 cm, and r = -1.0 for all other steps. This prevents reward hacking!

💡

2. Hindsight Experience Replay

When an episode fails to reach goal A but ends at point B, HER relabels point B as the desired goal! This turns 100% of failed exploration steps into positive training signals.

🧠

3. Soft Actor-Critic (SAC)

SAC optimizes maximum entropy RL, encouraging exploration while maintaining target temperature α. Combined with HER, SAC reaches 78%+ success rate!
