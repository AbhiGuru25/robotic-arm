Comparative Study of DDPG, TD3, SAC, and PPO for Sparse-Reward Robotic Arm Manipulation using HER

Abhi Virani • Dept. of Computer Science (AI & ML), Adani University
GitHub Repository Telemetry Simulator Benchmark Results

Abstract

Autonomous robotic manipulation in continuous action spaces with sparse rewards presents a fundamental challenge for model-free Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL). Under binary sparse rewards ($r = 0$ upon task completion, $r = -1$ otherwise), conventional exploration algorithms suffer from extreme sample inefficiency and failure to compute non-zero gradients. In this paper, we present a comparative empirical study evaluating four landmark DRL algorithms—Deep Deterministic Policy Gradient (DDPG), Twin Delayed DDPG (TD3), Soft Actor-Critic (SAC), and Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO)—augmented with Hindsight Experience Replay (HER) on a 7-DOF Franka Emika Panda robotic arm executing goal-conditioned pick-and-place tasks (PandaPickAndPlace-v3). Over 1,000,000 training steps, our proposed SAC + HER architecture achieves a peak success rate of 78.0%, demonstrating superior exploration and sample efficiency over deterministic off-policy baselines.

System Architecture & Methodology

Mathematical formulation of goal-conditioned RL and hindsight experience augmentation

01 | SPARSE BINARY REWARD

Reward Formulation

The agent operates under a strict binary indicator function:

$r(s, a, g) = -\mathbb{I}(\|g - s_{\text{achieved}}\|_2 > \epsilon)$

Reward $r=0$ is granted only when the object position is within tolerance threshold $\epsilon = 5\text{ cm}$ of goal $g$.

02 | HINDSIGHT REPLAY

HER Sampling Strategy

Re-labels unachieved trajectory states using the future strategy ($k=4$ ratio):

$g' \sim \text{AchievedStates}(s_{t' > t})$

Transforms failed trajectory attempts into successful training signals by substituting virtual goals.

03 | SAC OPTIMIZATION

Maximum Entropy RL

Optimizes both expected cumulative return and policy entropy:

$J(\pi) = \mathbb{E}\left[ \sum r_t + \alpha \mathcal{H}(\pi(\cdot|s_t)) \right]$

Automated temperature tuning ($\alpha$) maintains robust exploration in continuous action spaces.
