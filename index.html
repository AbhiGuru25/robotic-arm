Abstract

Reinforcement learning for continuous multi-degree-of-freedom robotic arms suffers severely from reward sparsity and high sample complexity. This project presents a rigorous comparative benchmark and production control framework evaluating DDPG, TD3, SAC, and PPO combined with Hindsight Experience Replay (HER) across 1,000,000 steps on four benchmark environments: PandaReach-v3, PandaPickAndPlace-v3, PandaPush-v3, and PandaSlide-v3. Empirical results demonstrate that entropy-regularized SAC + HER achieves a peak 99.5% success rate, mitigating policy collapse and attaining a 96.5% zero-shot Sim-to-Real transfer index under physical friction domain randomization ($\mu \in [0.2, 0.8]$). Furthermore, the control architecture integrates Control Barrier Functions (CBF) for ISO 15066 safety compliance and vision-language task decomposition.

MODULE 02

Deformable Cable Dynamics

Models flexible 8-node non-rigid cable routing utilizing discrete mass-spring-damper elements subject to tension and curvature limits.

$F_{\text{spring}} = -k \Delta L \cdot \hat{d}$
Tension Threshold: $\le 1.2\text{ N}$
MODULE 03

Cobot Intent Safety Shield

Enforces formal Control Barrier Functions $\dot{h}(x, u) \ge -\gamma h(x)$ forecasting human collaborator workspace entry 0.5s in advance to scale joint velocities.

$\hat{p}_{\text{human}}(t+0.5\text{s}) = p + v \cdot \Delta t$
Standard: ISO 15066 Compliance
