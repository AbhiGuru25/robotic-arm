GitHub Repository Multi-Task Simulator Benchmark Results

Abstract

Autonomous robotic manipulation across diverse multi-task environments with sparse binary rewards poses severe sample inefficiency challenges for model-free Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL). In this work, we present a multi-task empirical benchmark evaluating Soft Actor-Critic (SAC), Twin Delayed DDPG (TD3), DDPG, and PPO augmented with Hindsight Experience Replay (HER) across four 7-DOF Franka Emika Panda manipulation benchmarks: PandaReach-v3, PandaPickAndPlace-v3, PandaPush-v3, and PandaSlide-v3. Our proposed SAC + HER architecture achieves 99.5% success on Reach, 78.0% on Pick & Place, 84.5% on Push, and 68.0% on Slide over 1,000,000 training steps, establishing state-of-the-art sample efficiency on goal-conditioned tasks.

System Architecture & Methodology

Mathematical formulation of goal-conditioned RL and hindsight experience augmentation

01 | SPARSE BINARY REWARD

Reward Formulation

The agent operates under a strict binary indicator function:

$r(s, a, g) = -\mathbb{I}(\|g - s_{\text{achieved}}\|_2 > \epsilon)$

Reward $r=0$ is granted only when object position is within tolerance threshold $\epsilon = 5\text{ cm}$ of goal $g$.

02 | HINDSIGHT REPLAY

HER Sampling Strategy

Re-labels unachieved trajectory states using the future strategy ($k=4$ ratio):

$g' \sim \text{AchievedStates}(s_{t' > t})$

Transforms failed trajectory attempts into successful training signals by substituting virtual goals.

03 | SAC OPTIMIZATION

Maximum Entropy RL

Optimizes both expected cumulative return and policy entropy:

$J(\pi) = \mathbb{E}\left[ \sum r_t + \alpha \mathcal{H}(\pi(\cdot|s_t)) \right]$

Automated temperature tuning ($\alpha$) maintains robust exploration in continuous action spaces.
