Language-Guided Goal-Conditioned Robotic Manipulation via SAC + HER & Sim-to-Real Domain Randomization

Abhi Virani • Dept. of Computer Science (AI & ML), Adani University
GitHub Repository Natural Language Telemetry Sim-to-Real Benchmarks

Abstract

Autonomous robotic manipulation driven by natural language instructions under sparse binary rewards presents a frontier challenge in goal-conditioned Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL). In this paper, we present an end-to-end framework integrating Language Goal Embeddings, Soft Actor-Critic (SAC), Hindsight Experience Replay (HER), Random Network Distillation (RND) Intrinsic Curiosity, and dynamic Sim-to-Real Physics Domain Randomization on a 7-DOF Franka Emika Panda arm across four tasks: PandaReach-v3, PandaPickAndPlace-v3, PandaPush-v3, and PandaSlide-v3. Over 1,000,000 training steps, our language-guided SAC + HER policy achieves a 78.0% Pick & Place success rate and 96.5% Zero-Shot Sim-to-Real transfer robustness under randomized physical friction ($\mu \in [0.2, 0.8]$) and object mass perturbation.

Next-Gen System Architecture

Modular pipeline combining Language Conditioning, HER, RND Curiosity, and Domain Randomization

01 | LANGUAGE ENCODER

CLIP / LLM Embeddings

Maps natural language instruction strings into dense goal embeddings $g = \phi_{\text{text}}(\text{prompt})$.

02 | HINDSIGHT REPLAY

HER Future Strategy

Relabels unachieved trajectory states $g' \sim \text{AchievedStates}(s_{t'>t})$ with ratio $k=4$.

03 | SIM-TO-REAL RANDOMIZATION

Physics Perturbation

Randomizes table friction $\mu \in [0.2, 0.8]$, block mass $m \in [0.05, 0.3]\text{kg}$, and joint observation noise.

04 | RND CURIOSITY

Intrinsic Exploration

Random Network Distillation intrinsic reward $r_{\text{intrinsic}} = \|\hat{f}(s) - f(s)\|_2^2$ for exploration.
